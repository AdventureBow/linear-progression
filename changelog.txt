Linear Progression — Changelog
==============================
Pack: linear_progression (Minecraft 1.21.11, pack_format 94.1)
Scope: everything changed in the development session ending 2026-08-11


## Chainmail

- Passive sat drip while wearing chainmail (survival/adventure only)
  - Uses the saturation effect (player food NBT is not writable in vanilla)
  - Intervals by piece count: 1 → 15s, 2 → 10s, 3 → 4.5s, 4 → 1s
- Movement speed +5% per piece (half of gold armor’s 10%)
  - Item attribute stamp with marker `lp_chain_speed`
  - Worn pieces stamped on equip/tick path; carry inventory stamped on inventory change


## Turtle shell

- Infinite water breathing while worn
  - Finite potion WB saved on equip and restored on unequip
- Item stamp: water movement efficiency 0.3 (`lp_turtle`)
- While wet (water/bubble at feet or eyes): movement speed +50%


## Netherite fire resistance

- Infinite fire resistance when netherite armor pieces + netherite trims ≥ 4
- Finite potion FR saved on equip and restored on unequip (same idea as turtle WB)
- Armor-granted FR uses hidden particles
- Replaced the old 1-second re-apply path; removed dead/no-op FR helpers


## keepInventory curse handling

With `keepInventory` on, vanilla curses break; death cleanup now:

- Curse of Binding: stripped from the four armor slots on death
  - Same temp-item + data-remove method as the working blessing potion prototype
  - Re-runs once the player is alive again if death-time item ops fail
- Curse of Vanishing: any vanishing-cursed item is deleted on death
  - Inventory, hotbar, armor, offhand, mainhand, cursor
  - Also re-runs on first alive tick after death


## Inventory stamping refactor

- Unified stamp helpers under `global_systems/stamp/`:
  - Shared one-slot macro, carry-slot scanner, worn-only gold/chainmail paths
  - Early-out when no relevant items are present
  - Split worn (equip) vs carry (bag/hotbar) stamping
- Rewrote gold armor, chainmail speed, turtle helmet, and edible food stamping on the helper
- Large size cuts (e.g. edible stamp ~421 → ~59 lines)
- Fixed GS stamps failing to load after an invalid `execute unless function … with storage` early-out
- Gold unequip now only clears equip tags (speed/KB live on item components)
- Removed dead paths: entity gold attribute removes, `stamp_gold_kb_armor`, netherite FR redirect/no-op files
- New item tags: `chainmail_armor`, `stamp_edibles`
